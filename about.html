ABOUT CROP AI

Building a smarter field of vision.

CropAI is designed as an AI-powered crop health platform that connects computer vision with an intuitive agricultural interface.

THE IDEA

Agriculture generates visual signals.

Leaves can reveal visible signs of plant stress, infection and disease. CropAI explores how computer vision can transform these visual signals into accessible AI-assisted information.

The goal is to make crop analysis more approachable through a simple image-first interface.

THE VISION

Intelligence should be easy to access.

A complex machine-learning pipeline should not require a complicated user experience. CropAI therefore focuses on turning technical AI processes into a clean and understandable workflow.
