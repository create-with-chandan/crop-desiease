Better input. Better insight.

01

Use natural light

Capture the leaf in clear lighting so visible spots, patterns and texture remain easy to analyze.

02

Keep the leaf visible

Avoid unnecessary background objects. A centered leaf can provide a cleaner visual signal.

03

Review the result
